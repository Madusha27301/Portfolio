The Impact of Financial Leverage on Firm Profitability: An Empirical Study of Sri Lankan Healthcare Companies (2020-2024)

W.G.K.M. Deshan, Sulanie Perera, Tiloka De Silva

This research examines the impact of financial leverage on the profitability of healthcare companies listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) during and post-COVID-19 era. Using panel data techniques, the study analyzes how capital structure decisions influence ROA and ROE, highlighting the significance of Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR) in driving financial performance.
